## SuperStore Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was developed as part of the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship (Week 2).\
The goal was to design an executive dashboard in **Power BI** that helps management monitor sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and regional performance.

## 🛠 Business Problem

Retail companies often struggle to track sales, profit margins, customer segments, and regional performance in one place.\
This dashboard solves that problem by transforming raw SuperStore data into actionable insights.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- **KPI Cards**: Total Sales (\$2.30M), Total Profit (\$286.40K), Total Orders (5K), Average Sales (\$229.86), Profit Margin (\~12%).\
- **Visualizations**:
  - Regional Sales & Profit
  - Profit Margin by Region
  - Category-Level Sales vs Profit
  - Profit Contribution by Sub-Category
  - Yearly Sales & Profit Trend (2014–2017)
  - Customer Segment Sales Share (Donut Chart)
  - Top 5 Products Driving Profit (Table)
- **Slicers/Filters**: Region, Segment, Category, Ship Date, Year, Ship Mode.

## 🔎 Key Insights

1.  Strong sales but modest profit margins (\~12%).\
2.  West region leads in both sales and profitability; Central and South lag behind.\
3.  Consumer segment contributes over 50% of revenue, creating dependency.\
4.  Technology drives sales, but Furniture struggles with margins.\
5.  Copiers dominate product profitability, led by Canon imageCLASS 2200.

## ⚠️ Risks

- Overdependence on Consumer segment.\
- Low overall profit margin.\
- Regional imbalance (Central and South underperforming).

## 🌱 Opportunities

- Expand copier product line.\
- Grow Corporate and Home Office segments.\
- Target promotions in weaker regions.

## 🧩 Skills Developed

- Data cleaning & transformation with Power Query.\
- KPI creation and DAX measures (Profit Margin, Total Sales, etc.).\
- Dashboard design and storytelling in Power BI.\
- Communicating insights into risks and opportunities.
